Created a faulty Employee Management program with 2 intentional bugs:
Bonus calculation used salary * 10 instead of salary * 0.10.
Salary update used salary - increment instead of salary + increment.
Used Delve (dlv) to debug the program.

Commands used
-------------
dlv debug .
break calculateBonus
continue
print salary
next
print bonus

This helped identify the incorrect bonus value: 200000 instead of 2000.

Then:

break updateSalary
continue
print employee
print increment
next
print employee.Salary
next
print employee.Salary

This showed that the salary changed from 20000 to 15000, confirming the second bug.

After identifying both issues, the faulty statements can be corrected and the program tested again using:

go run .

Result: Debugging helped identify the exact lines where the incorrect calculations were happening.
