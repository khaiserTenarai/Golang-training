Different Log Levels
Definition:
Log levels are used to classify log messages based on their importance or severity.

Why do we use them?
INFO → tells us what the application is normally doing.

WARNING → indicates something unusual that may need attention.

ERROR → indicates that something went wrong.

For example:

INFO: application started
WARNING: salary is below expected value
ERROR: database connection failed

Purpose: Log levels make it easier to understand, filter, and troubleshoot application logs, especially when there are many log messages.
